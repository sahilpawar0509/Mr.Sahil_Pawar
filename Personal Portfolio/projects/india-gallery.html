India Image Gallery.

Discover the beauty of India through a collection of images and information about popular places across the country.

OVERVIEW

About the Project.

India Image Gallery is a web-based project created to showcase the cultural, historical, and natural beauty of India.

The platform allows visitors to explore images of popular destinations and learn basic information about different places across the country.

The main objective is to present India's diversity through an organised and visually engaging gallery.

PROJECT DETAILS

Key Highlights.

  • Collection of images featuring popular places in India.
  • Information about selected destinations.
  • Visual exploration of India's cultural and natural beauty.
  • Organised gallery-based presentation.
  • Designed to make exploring places simple and engaging.
OBJECTIVE

Purpose of the Project.

The project aims to create an accessible visual experience where users can discover interesting destinations in India. It also provides practical experience in building and organising a web-based gallery.
