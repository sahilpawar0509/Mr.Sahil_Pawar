HACKATHON TEAM PROJECT

HeatCare AI

Predicting hospital demand during extreme heat to help healthcare facilities prepare beds, staff, and essential resources.

OVERVIEW

About the Project

HeatCare AI is a team hackathon project focused on predicting hospital demand during periods of extreme heat.

Extreme temperatures can increase pressure on healthcare facilities. Hospitals need to understand possible changes in patient demand so they can prepare their resources in advance.

The project uses historical hospital attendance information and weather-related data to estimate future patient demand and present useful information through a dashboard.

CORE FEATURES

Key Highlights

  • Predicts upcoming hospital patient demand.
  • Considers historical hospital attendance data.
  • Uses temperature and humidity as weather-related inputs.
  • Displays predicted demand through a dashboard.
  • Helps compare expected demand with available hospital capacity.
  • Supports planning for beds, staff, and essential resources.

OBJECTIVE

Purpose of the Project

The main objective is to help hospitals prepare for possible increases in patient demand during extreme heat conditions. Better demand estimates can support resource planning and help healthcare teams make more informed operational decisions.

MY EXPERIENCE

Working on HeatCare AI

This project provided experience in collaborative hackathon development, working with healthcare datasets, connecting backend services with a dashboard, and exploring demand forecasting for a real-world problem.
